# Flocus - Stay Focused & Mindful

**Flocus** (formerly Flucos) is a powerful Android productivity app designed to help you reclaim your focus and eliminate digital distractions. By creating a dedicated focus environment, Flocus helps you stay committed to your tasks without the constant urge to check your phone.

## 🚀 Features

- **Session Timer:** Set a goal and watch the time count down in a distraction-free interface.
- **Strict & Standard Modes:** Choose your level of focus. Standard mode allows selected apps, while Strict mode blocks everything.
- **App Whitelisting:** Select specific apps (like Music, Calculator, or Notes) that remain accessible during your focus sessions.
- **Overlay Protection:** A persistent overlay ensures you stay within the app and don't wander off to social media.
- **Emergency Escapes:** Limited weekly "escapes" for urgent situations, discouraging casual session ending.
- **Lock Screen Integration:** Works over the lock screen to keep you focused from the moment you wake your device.
- **Analytics & History:** Track your focus progress over time (coming soon/in-progress).
- **Scheduled Alarms:** Integrate focus sessions with your daily routine.

## 🛠️ Permissions Used

To provide a robust focus experience, Flocus requires several specialized Android permissions:
- **System Alert Window:** To display the focus overlay over other apps.
- **Usage Stats:** To detect and block unauthorized app usage during sessions.
- **Notification Policy:** To manage "Do Not Disturb" settings automatically.
- **Foreground Service:** To ensure your session isn't killed by the system in the background.
